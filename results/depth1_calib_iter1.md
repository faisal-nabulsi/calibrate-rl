## Key structural finding that shaped the edits
Many chains' difficulty AND their answer-diversity are feeder-bound: a feeder that
emits only a handful of distinct intermediates V caps the composite's text
diversity. The dedupe gate (survival >= 0.90, by problem text) is therefore the
binding constraint, not the math. Consequence: shrinking a choice-pool to ease a
chain backfires - fewer target-clause variants -> more text collisions -> dedupe
fails. So I eased only where diversity had headroom, and diversified the
low-diversity targets by widening the non-fed input pools (both the LOW_DIVERSITY
direction and a dedupe win).
