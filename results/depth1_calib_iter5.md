# depth-1 calibration — iter5 edits & rationale

Baseline (iter5 readout): 0/47 in-band, mean goldilocks-frac 0.07, 47 flagged.
All edits are **chain-layer only** (22 chain_*.json knob files + one guarded adapter
in `_a_equalize_g`). No depth-0 atom or knobs/<atomic>.json touched -> atom-equivalence
test **PASSES (4/4)**. No check_dataset.py change needed: every chain recomputer reads
the difficulty params straight from the clause **text** (regex), so any in-envelope knob
value or the new `fn` pool is recomputed correctly with no recomputer edit.

## Gate replication (the binding constraint)
The campaign gate (tools/depth1_calib_campaign.py:gate) hard-fails -- and **reverts the
WHOLE iteration** -- if ANY chain has top3 > 0.50 or dedupe < 0.40. The strict 0.30/0.90
in static_checks.py is advisory; only SEVERE collapse reverts. Verified locally over all
47 chains (n=40/chain, 1636 golds): **0 gold mismatches, 0 SEVERE chains**, and re-measured
every edited chain at n=200 (max top3 = 0.385, min dedupe = 0.64 -- comfortable margins).

The readout per-chain top3 (0.33-1.0) is **n=2-10 noise** -- at ~5 samples a single repeat
forces top3>=0.4. So a LOW_DIVERSITY verdict on a chain whose **mean is already ~0.45-0.55**
is a sampling artifact, not a real answer-hack. I acted on the **mean** (the binding GRPO
signal) and on build-time diversity at n=200, not the readout top3.
